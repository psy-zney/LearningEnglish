# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are people actively learning English, likely self-directed learners who want to collect new vocabulary, review it over time, and practice recall. Secondary users are administrators or teachers who curate vocabulary entries, correct meanings, and trigger AI-assisted checks.

## Product Purpose

This product helps learners build English vocabulary through capture, review, and practice. It combines a vocabulary list, spaced repetition scheduling, pronunciation support, and AI-generated practice so learning stays active instead of passive. Success means users return regularly, add words quickly, and can see clear progress in what they know, what needs review, and what they are still learning.

## Brand Personality

Helpful, practical, encouraging.

The tone should feel like a focused study companion: calm, clear, and efficient, with enough warmth to make practice feel manageable rather than academic or intimidating.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards with empty hero stats, noisy neon AI branding, or school-like worksheets that feel punitive. Do not make the interface look like a toy flashcard app, a corporate admin panel, or a consumer social feed.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next learning action obvious, especially review and practice.
2. Show progress and correction clearly so users can trust the system.
3. Keep vocabulary entry and editing fast, low-friction, and forgiving.
4. Use AI as a teaching aid, not as a flashy centerpiece.
5. Preserve focus by limiting visual noise and unnecessary branching paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA behavior where practical. Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, clear error states, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Treat English and Vietnamese text carefully, avoid relying on color alone for status, and ensure pronunciation and practice flows remain usable without sound.
